# Scope, limitations and safe use

BedsideLogic is an AI-assisted nursing documentation and clinical-workflow prototype. It is not a validated medical device or a substitute for a qualified clinician's assessment, local procedures, or clinical judgement.

- Do not use generated text as a diagnosis, prescription, triage decision, or independently verified clinical record.
- Review and correct all generated content before any clinical use.
- Do not enter identifiable patient information unless the specific deployment, data flow, access controls, legal basis, and organizational approval explicitly permit it.
- A prototype UI or API integration does not establish regulatory compliance, clinical effectiveness, security certification, or production readiness.
- Automated checks cover only the behaviors they test; passing tests do not prove clinical safety or absence of vulnerabilities.
- Integrations, persistence, access controls, audit trails, and export paths must be verified in the actual deployment before being relied upon.

This repository is engineering and workflow demonstration material. The operator remains responsible for safe configuration, human review, privacy protection, and compliance with applicable requirements.
